Ceremony background — the dithered horizon

One object, anchored off the bottom edge, dead still unless the user is doing something. Measured off inspo/ScreenRecording…MP4: FocusFlight's background sits at a frame-to-frame delta of 0.000 when idle, jumps to 10–17 during a gesture, and returns to 0.000 the instant the finger lifts.

It is one field with one driver. The dither is a filter on it, not a second background: every step leaves it on except breathe, which drops to the smooth band of light because a breath needs something continuous to follow. The switch happens under the blur dissolve, so the two renders are never seen on top of each other.

Drag up and down on the phone to drive the field by hand — that is the 1:1 gesture coupling. Then turn on Ambient drift to feel what FocusFlight deliberately refuses.

Home is still by design. Nothing moves until you do.

The field, per step

  • Home, exercise, hum — dithered. The horizon quantised through an ordered 8×8 Bayer matrix at a w/112 dot pitch. Revives the parked dot-dither identity without the orator figure that was the actual problem, and kills gradient banding on OLED black.
  • Breathe — the smooth band of light. Same geometry, same driver, dither off. The dots quantise growth into visible steps, which fights a four-second inhale; the continuous glow is the pacer.
  • The handoff is the shipped blur dissolve — 28pt radius, 280ms, 45% out then 55% in, with the render swapped unanimated at the midpoint. Same move as the text.

Behaviour in both renders

  • Still at rest. No ambient loop. A background that always moves cannot respond.
  • Brightness rises with commitment. Measured arc in the reference: 33.5 at home → 80.8 once committed → 107.4 in the session, with deliberate dark dips at the in-between beats.
  • Never a figure, a stage, or an audience. The constraint from docs/scoping-entry-ritual.md that ruled out the orator plates.
  • Text changes dissolve through blur at 0.4 × type size over 280ms, as shipped on this branch.
