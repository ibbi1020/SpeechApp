The veil

Same wash as pause. A gray gradient, not black, climbs from the bottom and stops at three quarters of the screen. The bottom leads. The edge stays soft, so it does not turn into a slab. The report behind it blurs a little while the wash is up.

The words come in and leave through blur, the same move as a line of copy changing. Nothing slides. The wash drops only after the words are gone.
