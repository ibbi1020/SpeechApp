Filter Progress by day

All time is the default. Tap a day for that day. Tap a second day, or drag across days, for a stretch. One highlight stretches across those days. Clear removes the selection.

Ease

One highlight eases out to the day you choose. It always takes the same time.

Weight

One highlight is heavy. It lags behind your finger, then settles. It does not bounce.

Reach

One highlight follows your finger, then settles.

A check is a saved session. A dot is a day since the first session with nothing saved. A faint dot is later this month. The ring is today. Future days do not select. Sounds, skipped words, and reading speed have no date in the app yet. These pictures use sample sessions so the filter is visible. Turns and long silences already have a date on the recording.
